# FATF 40 Recommendations — Interactive Reference & Learning Platform

An interactive, bilingual (**English & Bahasa Indonesia**) reference and study platform covering the **Financial Action Task Force (FATF) International Standards on Combating Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism & Proliferation** (updated through the **June 2026 Edition**).

Designed for both **newcomers / general learners** and **AML/CFT compliance practitioners, auditors, regulators, and law enforcement officers**, the platform bridges technical regulatory standards with practical illustrations, operational scenarios, and real-world global enforcement case studies.

---

## Core Objectives

1. **Make the 40 FATF Recommendations Accessible to Anyone**:
   - Every recommendation includes a **Practical Guide** (*Panduan Dasar*) featuring an everyday illustration, a step-by-step operational scenario, a plain-language terminology breakdown, and a *Misconception vs. Standard* comparison.
2. **Preserve Global Terminology while Explaining in Natural Bahasa Indonesia**:
   - In Bahasa Indonesia (`ID`) mode, international institutions, regulatory concepts, and acronyms are presented in their original English form alongside their Indonesian translation—for example:
     - *Financial Action Task Force (FATF) atau Badan Penentu Standar Global Anti Kejahatan Keuangan*
     - *Risk-Based Approach (RBA) atau Pendekatan Berbasis Risiko*
     - *Customer Due Diligence (CDD) atau Prinsip Mengenali Pengguna Jasa (PMPJ)*
     - *Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) atau Laporan Transaksi Keuangan Mencurigakan (LTKM)*
     - *Beneficial Owner (BO) atau Pemilik Manfaat Sebenarnya*
3. **Connect Rules to Real-World Global Breaches**:
   - Each of the 40 Recommendations is paired with an in-depth **Practitioner Dossier & Global Case Study** detailing historical enforcement actions, institutional failures, criminal typologies, and FATF Mutual Evaluation (*Assessor's Lens*) criteria.

## Key Features

### 1. Foundation Journey (`S0` – `S3`)
A 4-step introductory curriculum for learners starting from zero:
- **S0 — Threat Landscape**: The core triad of **Money Laundering (ML)** (Placement, Layering, Integration), **Terrorist Financing (TF)**, and **Proliferation Financing (PF)**.
- **S1 — Global Network**: How the **FATF**, the **9 FATF-Style Regional Bodies (FSRBs)** (such as APG), the **Mutual Evaluation (MER)** peer-review process, and the **Grey List / Black List** work across 200+ jurisdictions.
- **S2 — Evolution of the Standards**: Timeline from the 1989 G7 Paris Summit and the 1990 original 40 Recommendations through the 2012 unified revision, 2019 Virtual Assets (R.15), 2022–2023 Beneficial Ownership (R.24/R.25), June 2025 Payment Transparency (R.16), and June 2026 Humanitarian Exemption (INR.6) updates.
- **S3 — Structural Architecture**: How the Recommendations, **29 Binding Interpretive Notes (IN)**, and the **FATF Glossary** interact legally.

### 2. Interactive Architecture Map (Sections `A` – `G`)
Full coverage of all **40 Recommendations** organized across the 7 official structural pillars:
- **Section A (R.1 – R.2)**: AML/CFT Policies and Coordination
- **Section B (R.3 – R.8)**: Money Laundering and Confiscation, Terrorist Financing & Proliferation Financing
- **Section C (R.9 – R.23)**: Preventive Measures for Financial Institutions (`FI`) and Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (`DNFBPs` / Gatekeepers)
- **Section D (R.24 – R.25)**: Transparency and Beneficial Ownership of Legal Persons and Arrangements
- **Section E (R.26 – R.35)**: Powers and Responsibilities of Competent Authorities (Supervisors, FIUs, Law Enforcement) and Other Institutional Measures
- **Section F (R.36 – R.40)**: International Cooperation (Conventions, Mutual Legal Assistance, Extradition, and Agency-to-Agency Intelligence Sharing)
- **Section G**: The 21 Mandatory Designated Categories of Predicate Offences & Official Thresholds

### 3. 5-Minute Daily Quick Study Mode
An adaptive daily micro-learning generator that builds a 5-minute study plan based on user progress:
- **4 Study Focus Tracks**:
  1. *Smart Adaptive*: Automatically prioritizes unvisited recommendations and unanswered quizzes.
  2. *Unexplored Areas*: Focuses on sections the learner has not yet opened.
  3. *Quiz Mastery*: Targets recommendations visited but not yet tested.
  4. *2025–2026 Revisions*: Focuses on recently updated standards (`R.1`, `R.6`, `R.8`, `R.15`, `R.16`, `R.24`, `R.25`).
- **3 Timed Stages**:
  - **Stage 1 (2 Min)**: Core Concepts, Practical Illustrations & Global Case Snapshots for 3 selected recommendations.
  - **Stage 2 (1 Min)**: Key Regulatory Thresholds & Glossary Drill.
  - **Stage 3 (2 Min)**: 3-Question Rapid Verification Sprint.

### 4. Deep-Dive Slide Viewer & Practitioner Dossier
Every Recommendation (`R.1` – `R.40`) includes:
- **Core Essence & Mandatory Obligations**: Structured breakdown of sub-obligations and binding Interpretive Note highlights.
- **Practical Guide (*Panduan Dasar*)**:
  1. Practical Illustration (*Gambaran Praktis*)
  2. Operational Scenario (*Contoh Penerapan di Lapangan*)
  3. Key Terminology (*Penjelasan Istilah Teknis*)
  4. Common Misconception (*Meluruskan Salah Kaprah*)
- **12 Interactive Compliance Diagrams**: Interactive visual simulations for `R.1` (RBA), `R.3` (21 Predicate Categories), `R.6` (Targeted Financial Sanctions), `R.10` (CDD Triggers), `R.16` (Wire Transfer Travel Rule), `R.22` (DNFBP Gatekeepers), `R.24` (Beneficial Ownership Piercing), `R.29` (FIU Hub), `R.32` (Cross-Border Cash Couriers), `R.36` (UN Conventions), `R.37` (MLA Pipeline), and `R.40` (Horizontal/Diagonal Cooperation).
- **4-Tab Practitioner Dossier**:
  - *Tab 1*: Operational Reality (*Why It Exists & Monday Morning Compliance Reality*)
  - *Tab 2*: Real-World Global Breach Case Study (Jurisdiction, What Happened, The Breach, Consequences & Lessons)
  - *Tab 3*: Criminal Playbook & Typology Red Flags
  - *Tab 4*: FATF Mutual Evaluation Assessor's Lens
- **Scenario Quiz**: Interactive comprehension verification with detailed explanations.

### 5. Crosswalk & Quick Reference Tables
- **2003 → 2012 Crosswalk Table**: Maps legacy 40 Recommendations + 9 Special Recommendations (`IX SR`) to the current 1–40 numbering.
- **Mandatory Monetary Thresholds Reference**:
  - **$15,000 / €15,000**: Occasional transactions CDD (`R.10`), Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones cash transactions (`R.22`).
  - **$10,000 / €10,000**: Physical cross-border currency / BNI declaration or disclosure (`R.32`).
  - **$3,000 / €3,000**: Casino customer due diligence threshold (`R.22`).
  - **$1,000 / €1,000**: Qualifying wire transfers (`R.16`) and Virtual Asset transfers / Crypto Travel Rule (`R.15`).

### 6. Interactive Glossary & Progress Tracker
- Searchable and filterable glossary of core FATF legal and operational definitions, including the full list of **21 Designated Categories of Offences**.
- Persistent client-side progress tracking (`localStorage`) recording visited modules, passed quizzes, bookmarked recommendations, and completed daily study sessions.

## Getting Started Locally

1. **Install dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

2. **Start the development server** (runs on port `3000`):
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

3. **Build for production**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
